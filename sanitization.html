Your Safety, Our Priority

Sterilization & Infection Control in Tysons Corner

A clean, safe environment is the foundation of great dentistry. At Westpark Dental Studio, infection control isn't an afterthought — it's built into everything we do.

Clean by design

A dedicated sterilization center.

Our studio was purpose-built with a separate, dedicated sterilization suite where every instrument is cleaned, sterilized, and sealed under carefully controlled conditions. It's a space designed for one thing: keeping you safe.

We meet — and hold ourselves to — the infection-control standards set by the CDC, OSHA, and the ADA, so you can relax knowing your care is delivered in a genuinely clean, considered environment.

How we keep you safe

Protection at every step of your visit.

Steam Autoclave Sterilization

Every reusable instrument is heat-sterilized in a medical-grade autoclave that destroys bacteria, viruses, and spores.

Ultrasonic Pre-Cleaning

Instruments are ultrasonically cleaned to remove all debris before sterilization — nothing is sterilized dirty.

Single-Use Disposables

Wherever possible we use single-use, disposable items that are opened in front of you and never reused.

Surface Disinfection

Every operatory is thoroughly disinfected between patients, with barriers on high-touch surfaces.

Sterile Instrument Storage

Sterilized instruments are sealed in pouches and stored in dedicated cabinetry until the moment of use.

Treated Water Lines

Our dental unit waterlines are regularly treated and monitored to keep the water clean and safe.

Hand Hygiene & PPE

Our team follows strict hand-hygiene and personal-protective-equipment protocols throughout your visit.

Ongoing Monitoring

Our sterilizers are routinely tested and documented so our standards stay verifiably high.

Care you can feel good about

Comfortable, clean, and truly cared for.
