Same-Day Restorations · CEREC

Your crown, designed and placed in a single visit.

With our CEREC Primemill — paired with a Primescan digital scanner and a rapid chairside furnace — we can design, mill, and place a wide range of custom restorations without a second appointment. A crown can be milled in roughly five to eight minutes, then refined and seated the same day.

  • Finished in a single visit — often in one to two hours
  • No goopy impressions — a quick, comfortable digital scan
  • No temporaries and no return trip
  • Precise, natural-looking ceramic that matches your smile

3D Imaging · CBCT & Panoramic

See more, treat better — with 3D imaging.

Our advanced extraoral system captures a complete view of your oral health in seconds. A 2D panoramic scan reveals all of your teeth, jaws, sinuses, and nasal pathways in a single sweeping image — ideal for tracking wisdom teeth, TMJ concerns, and hidden infection.

When more detail is needed, the system transitions to high-precision 3D Cone-Beam CT, reconstructing a sub-millimeter model of your anatomy that maps nerves and bone for predictable implant, oral-surgery, and root-canal planning.

  • Complete 2D panoramic overview of your whole mouth
  • High-precision 3D CBCT for complex treatment planning
  • Ultra-low-dose protocols for enhanced safety
  • Open, comfortable design — no bulky sensors in your mouth
