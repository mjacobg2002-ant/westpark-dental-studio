Payment & financing

Flexible options for every budget.

We believe quality dental care should be accessible. Our team keeps billing simple and transparent, and our Financial Coordinator is always glad to walk you through your options before treatment begins.

  • Most major PPO insurance accepted — and we file your claims for you.
  • Flexible payment plans to spread out the cost of larger treatments.
  • A self-pay discount for patients without dental insurance.
  • We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards.
  • Out-of-network help — if you're free to choose your dentist, we'll file for reimbursement.
