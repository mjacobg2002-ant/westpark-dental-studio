A different kind of dental experience

A studio built around the patient — not the procedure.

Westpark Dental Studio was founded on a simple idea: that dentistry can feel calm, personal, and genuinely caring. We take the time to understand each patient, explain every option clearly, and treat the people in our chair the way we would treat our own family.

The result is care grounded in integrity and ethics, paired with modern dentistry and a comfortable, unhurried atmosphere — an experience shaped to the standard our McLean and Tysons neighbors expect.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Seiren Ajineh, DDS

General & Cosmetic Dentistry · AEGD

Dr. Ajineh brings together clinical expertise and a genuinely personal approach to care. She completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery and an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Nova Southeastern University, and is an Invisalign Preferred Provider.

Her focus spans everyday general dentistry and refined cosmetic work — always centered on listening first, then designing care around each patient's goals.
