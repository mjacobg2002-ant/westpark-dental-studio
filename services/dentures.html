Comfortable, natural-looking dentures

Dentures remain one of the most effective and affordable ways to replace multiple missing teeth. Today's dentures are a world away from the bulky, artificial-looking sets of the past — modern materials and precise fabrication create replacements that look natural and feel secure.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, Dr. Seiren Ajineh offers full dentures to replace an entire arch of teeth and partial dentures to fill in gaps while preserving your remaining natural teeth. We offer conventional, immediate, and implant-supported options — the latter snapping securely onto implants so they never slip.

We take the time to get the fit, bite, and appearance right, so our McLean and Tysons Corner patients can eat their favorite foods, speak clearly, and smile with confidence again.

Questions & answers

Dentures in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Most patients adjust within a few weeks. Eating and speaking feel more natural with practice, and we'll make adjustments to maximize comfort.

What are implant-supported dentures?

These dentures snap securely onto dental implants so they never slip or click — far more stable than conventional dentures. Learn more on our implants page.

Will dentures change how I look?

Well-made dentures support your facial muscles and restore your smile, often making you look more youthful. We design yours to complement your features.

How do I care for my dentures?

Clean them daily, remove them at night unless advised otherwise, and keep regular checkups so we can ensure the fit stays comfortable.
