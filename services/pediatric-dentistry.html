Questions & answers

Pediatric Dentistry in Tysons Corner — FAQs

When should my child first see the dentist?

The general recommendation is by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits help prevent problems and build comfort.

What are dental sealants?

A thin protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent cavities. Learn more on our sealants page.

How can I prevent cavities in my child's teeth?

Regular checkups, fluoride, sealants, good brushing and flossing habits, and limiting sugary snacks all help. We'll coach your family.

Do you see the whole family?

Yes! As a family practice in Tysons Corner, we care for children and adults alike, so you can book everyone's visits together.
