Restore & protect your teeth

Rebuild teeth that are damaged or missing

When a tooth is cracked, badly decayed, or has lost a large amount of structure, a dental crown restores it. A crown is a custom 'cap' that fits over the entire tooth, restoring its strength, shape, and appearance while protecting what remains underneath — often the finishing touch after a root canal.

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring a natural-looking replacement tooth to the healthy teeth or implants on either side. It fills the gap, restores your ability to chew and speak clearly, and keeps neighboring teeth from shifting out of place.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, Dr. Seiren Ajineh crafts crowns and bridges from lifelike materials color-matched to your natural teeth. Our McLean and Tysons Corner patients enjoy restorations that are as durable as they are beautiful.

Questions & answers

Crowns & Bridges in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How long do crowns and bridges last?

With good care, crowns and bridges commonly last 10–15 years or longer. Regular checkups help maximize their lifespan.

Will my crown match my other teeth?

Yes. We color-match your restoration to your surrounding teeth so it blends in naturally and is virtually undetectable.

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

Often, yes. A tooth is more fragile after a root canal, and a crown protects it from fracture and restores full function.

Is a bridge or an implant better for me?

Both are excellent options. Bridges don't require surgery, while implants are freestanding and preserve bone. Dr. Ajineh will help you choose.
