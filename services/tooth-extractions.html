When keeping a tooth isn't the best option

We always aim to save your natural teeth — but sometimes a tooth is hopelessly damaged or decayed, and removing it is the healthiest choice. Extraction may be recommended for severe decay or infection, advanced gum disease, a badly fractured tooth, crowding, or problematic wisdom teeth.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, Dr. Seiren Ajineh performs extractions with a gentle, careful hand and thorough numbing, so the procedure is far more comfortable than most patients expect. We'll walk you through simple aftercare to ensure smooth, speedy healing.

Just as importantly, we'll discuss your options for replacing the tooth — such as a dental implant, bridge, or denture — so you can maintain a complete, healthy smile. Our goal is always your long-term oral health, delivered with compassion to our McLean and Tysons Corner patients.

Questions & answers

Tooth Extractions in Tysons Corner — FAQs

Does a tooth extraction hurt?

We numb the area thoroughly, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure — only gentle pressure. Any soreness afterward is manageable with simple aftercare.

What should I do after an extraction?

We'll give you detailed instructions — typically rest, avoiding straws and smoking, and eating soft foods for a day or two. Follow them closely for smooth healing.

Should I replace an extracted tooth?

Usually yes (except for some wisdom teeth). Replacing a tooth with an implant, bridge, or denture prevents shifting and bone loss. We'll discuss your options.

Can you help with wisdom teeth?

Yes. Dr. Ajineh can evaluate problematic wisdom teeth and remove them or refer you to a specialist when appropriate.
