Questions & answers

Emergency Dentist in Tysons Corner — FAQs

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling or an abscess, or any dental injury with bleeding or pain. When in doubt, call us.

My tooth was knocked out — what should I do?

Handle it by the crown (not the root), gently rinse it, and if possible place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call us immediately — time is critical.

Can you see me today?

We reserve time for emergencies and do our best to see patients in pain the same day. Call (703) 988-3384 as early as you can.

What if I have a severe toothache?

A persistent, severe toothache often signals infection that needs prompt care, such as a root canal. Call us right away so we can relieve your pain.
