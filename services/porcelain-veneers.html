The gold standard in smile design

One treatment, a completely new smile

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of your teeth — and they're one of the most transformative treatments in cosmetic dentistry. In just a few visits, veneers can repair larger chips and cracks, close gaps, cover stubborn stains, and reshape teeth for a beautifully even smile.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, Dr. Seiren Ajineh designs each veneer case around your face and goals. Every veneer is crafted to match the shape, shade, and translucency of natural teeth, so the result is stunning yet believable.

Veneers are a favorite among our McLean and Tysons Corner patients who want a dramatic, long-lasting upgrade with minimal fuss. With proper care, quality porcelain veneers can look beautiful for well over a decade.

Questions & answers

Porcelain Veneers in Tysons Corner — FAQs

Do veneers look fake?

Not when they're done well. Dr. Ajineh custom-designs each veneer's shape, shade, and translucency to match natural teeth for a beautiful but believable smile.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, quality porcelain veneers commonly last 10–15 years or more.

Is getting veneers painful?

Most patients feel little to no discomfort. We ensure you're fully numb during preparation, and any minor sensitivity afterward is temporary.

Are veneers right for me?

Veneers are ideal for stains, chips, gaps, and minor misalignment. At your consultation we'll confirm whether veneers or an alternative best fits your goals.
