Questions & answers

Teeth Whitening in Tysons Corner — FAQs

Is professional whitening safe?

Yes. When supervised by a dentist, whitening is safe and effective. Dr. Ajineh checks your oral health first and uses formulas that minimize sensitivity.

How white will my teeth get?

Most patients brighten several shades. Results vary based on the type and severity of staining, which we'll discuss at your visit.

Does whitening cause sensitivity?

Some patients notice mild, temporary sensitivity — far less than with over-the-counter kits. We can recommend products to manage it.

How long do results last?

With good home care and occasional touch-ups, results can last a year or more. Limiting coffee, tea, and red wine helps.
