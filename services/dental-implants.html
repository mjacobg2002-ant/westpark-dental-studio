Dental Implants in Tysons Corner, VA

Missing a tooth? Dental implants are the longest-lasting, most natural-looking tooth replacement available today — and they can last a lifetime.

Book an Appointment Call (703) 988-3384

The next best thing to natural teeth

Replace missing teeth for good

A dental implant is a small, biocompatible titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. Once placed, it fuses with your jawbone to provide a rock-solid foundation for a natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture. The result feels, functions, and looks just like a real tooth.

Unlike traditional bridges, implants don't rely on neighboring teeth for support, and unlike removable dentures, they never slip or click. Just as importantly, implants stimulate the jawbone — preventing the bone loss and 'sunken' facial appearance that often follows tooth loss.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, Dr. Seiren Ajineh uses 3D digital imaging to plan implant placement with precision. Whether you're missing one tooth, several, or need implant-supported dentures, we'll craft a solution built to last for our McLean and Tysons Corner patients.

  • Looks, feels & functions like a natural tooth
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care
  • Prevents jawbone loss from missing teeth
  • No slipping like removable dentures
  • Preserves your surrounding healthy teeth

Questions & answers

Dental Implants in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The attached crown may need replacement after many years.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients are surprised how comfortable the procedure is. The area is fully numbed during placement, and post-op discomfort is typically mild and short-lived.

Am I a candidate for implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are good candidates. Dr. Ajineh uses 3D imaging to evaluate your bone and can discuss options like bone grafting if needed.

How much do implants cost?

Cost depends on how many implants you need and the final restoration. We'll provide a clear estimate and discuss payment options at your consultation.
