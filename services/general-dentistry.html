The foundation of a healthy smile

Preventive care that keeps you out of the dental chair

The easiest dental problem to treat is the one that never happens. At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, preventive dentistry is the heart of everything we do. Regular professional cleanings and exams let Dr. Seiren Ajineh catch small issues — a tiny cavity, early gum inflammation, a worn filling — long before they turn into painful, expensive problems.

During your visit we gently clean your teeth and perform a thorough exam using 3D digital imaging that exposes you to far less radiation than traditional film. We also screen for oral cancer and check the health of your gums, bite, and existing dental work.

Most patients from McLean, Vienna, and greater Tysons Corner do best with a checkup and cleaning every six months. If you're overdue, there's no judgment here — just a warm, comfortable team ready to get your smile back on track.

Questions & answers

General & Preventive Dentistry in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How often should I see the dentist?

Most patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months. If you have gum disease or other risk factors, Dr. Ajineh may recommend more frequent visits.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major PPO dental plans and file your insurance for you. Preventive visits are fully or largely covered by most plans. See our insurance page.

I haven't been to the dentist in years — will you judge me?

Never. Our team is warm, gentle, and completely judgment-free. We'll help you get back to a healthy smile at your pace.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes. Our 3D digital imaging uses a fraction of the radiation of older film X-rays and gives Dr. Ajineh a far clearer picture of your oral health.
