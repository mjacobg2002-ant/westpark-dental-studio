Fillings that blend right in

When decay creates a cavity, a filling repairs the damage — removing the decay and restoring the tooth's strength, shape, and function. Caught early, a small filling is a quick, comfortable way to stop a cavity from growing into a bigger, costlier problem.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, we use tooth-colored composite fillings that are carefully matched to your natural tooth shade, so your restoration is virtually invisible. Unlike old metal amalgam, composite bonds directly to the tooth and looks completely natural — ideal for teeth that show when you smile.

Dr. Seiren Ajineh takes care to keep the process comfortable and efficient, so McLean and Tysons Corner patients can get in, get treated, and get back to their day with a healthy, natural-looking smile.

  • Stops decay from spreading
  • Natural, tooth-colored finish
  • Bonds directly to the tooth
  • Comfortable, efficient treatment
  • Restores full chewing strength

Questions & answers

Tooth-Colored Fillings in Tysons Corner — FAQs

Are your fillings mercury-free?

Yes. We use tooth-colored composite resin fillings — not metal amalgam — which look natural and bond directly to the tooth.

How do I know if I need a filling?

Common signs include sensitivity, a visible dark spot, or a rough or snagging edge — but many cavities cause no symptoms early on, which is why regular exams matter.

Does getting a filling hurt?

We numb the area thoroughly, so the procedure is comfortable. Any mild sensitivity afterward is temporary.

How long do fillings last?

With good oral hygiene, composite fillings can last many years. We check them at every visit to make sure they stay in great shape.
