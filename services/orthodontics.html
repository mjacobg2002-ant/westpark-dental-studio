A properly aligned smile

Move teeth into their ideal position

Orthodontic treatment gently guides teeth and jaws into proper alignment, correcting crowding, gaps, and bite problems. Beyond a more attractive smile, straighter teeth are easier to keep clean and can reduce uneven wear, jaw strain, and future dental problems.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, we offer a range of orthodontic solutions tailored to your needs. These include traditional braces to correct misalignment, palatal expanders to widen a narrow jaw, headgear for more significant jaw discrepancies, and clear aligners for a discreet option — plus Invisalign, delivered by Dr. Ajineh as a Certified Preferred Provider.

Whether you're considering treatment for your child or for yourself, Dr. Ajineh will evaluate your smile and recommend the most effective approach for our McLean and Tysons Corner patients.

Questions & answers

Braces & Orthodontics in Tysons Corner — FAQs

What's the right age for braces?

Many children benefit from an orthodontic evaluation around age 7, but treatment can begin at various ages. Adults can straighten their teeth too — it's never too late.

Braces or clear aligners — which is better?

It depends on your case and lifestyle. Braces handle complex movements well; clear aligners like Invisalign are discreet and removable. We'll help you decide.

What is a palatal expander?

It's an appliance that gently widens a narrow upper jaw, usually in children, to create space and improve the bite.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

It varies by case, typically ranging from several months to a couple of years. Dr. Ajineh will give you a personalized estimate.
