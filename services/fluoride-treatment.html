Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride Treatment in Tysons Corner, VA

A fast, painless application of concentrated fluoride that strengthens enamel and reduces the progression of cavities — for kids and adults alike.

Small treatment, big protection

Give your enamel a fighting chance

Fluoride is nature's cavity fighter. It strengthens tooth enamel and helps reverse the earliest stages of decay before a cavity ever forms. While it's found in most toothpaste and tap water, a professional fluoride treatment delivers a far more concentrated, protective dose.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, a fluoride treatment takes just a couple of minutes at the end of your cleaning. We apply a professional-grade fluoride varnish or gel that bonds to your enamel and continues protecting your teeth long after you leave.

Fluoride treatments are especially valuable for children, patients prone to cavities, those with sensitive teeth, and anyone with orthodontic appliances. It's a quick, affordable way to protect the smiles of McLean and Tysons Corner families.

  • Strengthens enamel against decay
  • Reduces cavity progression
  • Eases tooth sensitivity
  • Great for kids & cavity-prone adults
  • Fast, painless & affordable

Questions & answers

Fluoride Treatment in Tysons Corner — FAQs

Who should get fluoride treatments?

Children, patients prone to cavities, those with sensitivity, dry mouth, or braces benefit most — but many adults choose fluoride for extra protection.

Is fluoride safe?

Yes. Professional fluoride treatments are safe and effective when applied by a dental professional in the proper dose.

How long does it take?

Just a couple of minutes, typically at the end of your regular cleaning.

Will insurance cover it?

Fluoride is often covered for children and sometimes for adults. We'll check your benefits and let you know before treatment.
