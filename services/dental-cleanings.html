More than a polish

The cornerstone of a healthy mouth

A professional cleaning does what a toothbrush simply can't. Over time, plaque hardens into tartar (calculus) along the gumline and between teeth — and once it forms, only professional instruments can remove it. Left in place, it fuels cavities and gum disease.

At Westpark Dental Studio in Tysons Corner, your cleaning is gentle and thorough. We carefully remove plaque and tartar, polish away surface stains, and finish with tips tailored to your smile. Most patients leave with that unmistakable fresh, smooth feeling — and healthier gums.

Routine cleanings every six months are one of the best investments you can make in your long-term oral health. For patients with gum disease, we also offer deeper periodontal cleanings to get things back on track.

  • Removes plaque & hardened tartar
  • Fights cavities & gum disease
  • Removes surface stains for a brighter smile
  • Fresher breath & healthier gums

Questions & answers

Professional Teeth Cleanings in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How often do I need a cleaning?

Every six months for most patients. If you have gum disease, Dr. Ajineh may recommend more frequent periodontal maintenance cleanings.

Do cleanings hurt?

No — professional cleanings are gentle. If your gums are sensitive or inflamed, let us know and we'll take extra care to keep you comfortable.

What's the difference between a regular and a deep cleaning?

A regular cleaning maintains healthy teeth and gums. A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) treats gum disease by cleaning below the gumline. Learn more on our gum disease page.

Is a cleaning covered by insurance?

Most PPO dental plans cover two cleanings per year at little or no cost. We'll verify your benefits for you.
