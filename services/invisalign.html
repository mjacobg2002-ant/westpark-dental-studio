Clear. Comfortable. Removable.

A straighter smile — practically invisible

Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to gently move your teeth into their ideal position — no metal brackets, no wires, and no one needs to know you're straightening your smile. Because the aligners are removable, you can eat whatever you like and brush and floss normally.

As a Certified Invisalign Preferred Provider, Dr. Seiren Ajineh has the training and experience to treat a wide range of cases — from crowding and gaps to bite issues — right here in Tysons Corner. Using digital scans, we'll map your entire treatment and often show you a preview of your final smile before you begin.

Invisalign is a favorite among busy McLean and Tysons Corner professionals and teens alike who want a discreet, comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Most treatments finish in a matter of months, not years.

Questions & answers

Invisalign in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How long does Invisalign take?

Many cases finish in 6–18 months depending on complexity. Dr. Ajineh will give you a personalized timeline after your consultation and scan.

Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?

Invisalign is often comparable to traditional braces. We'll provide a clear estimate and discuss insurance and flexible payment options.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Aligners are far more comfortable than metal braces. You may feel mild pressure for a day or two after switching sets — a sign your teeth are moving as planned.

Can teens get Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign has options designed specifically for teens — a popular choice for busy Tysons Corner families.
