Dental Bonding

Dental Bonding in Tysons Corner, VA

The fast, affordable way to fix small chips, cracks, and gaps — often in a single visit, with no drilling and no downtime.

Quick fixes, beautiful results

Repair minor flaws in one visit

Dental bonding is one of the simplest and most cost-effective cosmetic treatments available. Using a tooth-colored composite resin, Dr. Seiren Ajineh can repair small chips and cracks, close minor gaps, reshape uneven teeth, and cover small stains — all in a single, comfortable visit.

The resin is carefully shaped, color-matched to your natural teeth, and hardened with a curing light, then polished to a natural shine. Because it usually requires little to no removal of tooth structure, most bonding is completed without anesthesia.

For McLean and Tysons Corner patients who want to fix a small imperfection quickly and affordably, bonding is often the perfect solution. For larger or more permanent changes, we'll talk through options like porcelain veneers.

Questions & answers

Dental Bonding in Tysons Corner — FAQs

How long does dental bonding last?

With good care, bonding typically lasts several years before needing a touch-up. Avoiding habits like nail-biting and ice-chewing helps it last longer.

Is bonding better than veneers?

They serve different needs. Bonding is faster and more affordable for small fixes; veneers are more durable and dramatic for full smile makeovers. We'll help you choose.

Does bonding stain?

Composite resin can stain over time more than porcelain. Limiting coffee, tea, and red wine — and regular cleanings — keeps it looking its best.

Does it hurt?

Usually not. Because bonding rarely removes tooth structure, most patients don't need any anesthesia.
